Summer 2026 Maker Camp

Real 3D Modeling Camp

A 5 day per week summer camp plan for a smart 10 year old who already sculpts in the real world and wants to learn real 3D modeling for 3D printing. Blender is the main tool. Fusion is introduced later for exact functional parts. Vacation is protected from May 28 through June 5.

Daily Camp Rhythm

The video tutorial is about 4 hours and 19 minutes. With pausing, building, fixing mistakes, and saving versions, it becomes roughly 7 to 8 hours of guided work. That is the spine, not the whole camp. The rest of the summer is original modeling, sculpting, print prep, functional design, rendering, and portfolio work.

5 to 15 minWatch or read only the section needed for today.
20 to 30 minSkill drill: one Blender skill, repeated until she can do it without panic.
30 to 60 minBuild time: apply the skill to her own model, not just the doughnut.
10 minSave a numbered version, export, screenshot, or write a camp note.
OptionalSlice or print only when the model is worth testing. Parent handles printer operations.

Parent Safety Rules

She can model, choose designs, inspect slicer previews, and keep the print log. Parent controls printer setup, hot parts, filament changes, failed print removal, blades, sanding, ventilation decisions, and account setup.

ToolRole
BlenderMain learning tool for sculpting, modeling, cleanup, rendering, and STL export.
PrusaSlicer or CuraSlicer for turning models into printable files and previewing problems before printing.
FusionLater add on for exact measurements, functional parts, clips, boxes, holders, and mechanical thinking.
